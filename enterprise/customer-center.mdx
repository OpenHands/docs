Use [Customer Center](https://customercenter.openhands.dev) to get the license key for your OpenHands Enterprise
installation, manage your subscription and invoices, and contact OpenHands Support.

Your Customer Center account is separate from the user accounts on your OpenHands Enterprise installation.

## Get an Account

You create a Customer Center account in one of two ways:

- **Start a trial**: Start a free 30-day trial on the [OpenHands pricing page](https://www.openhands.dev/pricing).
  Sign up with your work email address, then confirm it from the email we send you. Personal email domains, such as
  `gmail.com`, aren't accepted.
- **OpenHands Sales Creation**: We create your account and send you an invitation email. Use the link in the email
  to set your password. The link expires after 7 days. Ask your OpenHands contact to send a new one if it expires.

After you register, sign in with email you used to register the account. Each
Customer Center account has one sign-in. The person who uses it manages the license key and subscription.

## Get Your License Key

1. Sign in to Customer Center. You will land in the Overview page.
2. Under License key, click the copy icon to copy your key. If needed, Click the eye icon to show the full key.
3. Add the key to your installation. See [Configure License Key](/enterprise/license).

If you haven't installed OpenHands Enterprise yet, use the `Install` section on the same page. It has install
steps for [Docker Compose](/enterprise/docker-compose) and [Helm](/enterprise/k8s-install). Paste your license key when the setup wizard asks for it.

The top of the `Overview` page shows your account status, such as the days left in your trial, and your next step.

## Manage Billing

Use the `Billing` page to see your plan, seats, billing period, and invoices.

- **Subscribe**: During a trial, click `Subscribe` and pay through Stripe. You aren't charged until your trial ends.
- **Upgrade to Enterprise**: Click `Contact sales` on the OpenHands Enterprise plan.
- **Invoices**: Click `View` next to an invoice to open it.
- **Payment method and cancellation**: After you subscribe, update your payment method or cancel your subscription
  in Stripe from the `Billing` page. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

When a trial ends without a subscription, your license key stops working until you subscribe. If a payment fails,
your license key keeps working while Stripe retries the payment. Update your payment method to keep access.

If you bought OpenHands Enterprise through sales, your contract is billed outside Customer Center.

## Get Support

Use the `Support` page to reach OpenHands Support.

- **Contact support**: Click `Contact support` to email the support contact for your plan.
- **Send a support bundle**: Under `Support bundles`, click `Upload file` to send a support bundle or other files, up
  to 5 GB each. To create a support bundle, see
  [Troubleshooting](/enterprise/troubleshooting#start-with-a-support-bundle).

## Update Account Settings

Use the `Settings` page to change your name, company, email address, and password. To change your email address,
enter your current password to confirm.
